Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Housedex: More, then Upgrade to Housedex Pro, then Restore Purchases.

WageWindow: More, then Settings & Security, then Restore Purchase.

Waybill: Studio, then Upgrade to Waybill Pro, then Restore Purchases.

Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. We can't cancel subscriptions for you, because Apple handles billing.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Housedex and WageWindow can also sync between your own devices through your personal iCloud account. Waybill syncs only projects you choose to share with your team. We don't run servers that store your data. See each app's privacy policy below.

Why isn't iCloud syncing?

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on every device, that iCloud is turned on for the app in Settings, and that you have iCloud storage available. Give it a minute on a fresh install.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. Data already synced to your iCloud stays there until you remove it in Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage.
